Hardware partnership · Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Dhukra (Sirsa)

Help keep a real school robotics lab stocked.

About 100 students at Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Dhukra (SVM Dhukra), a small school in Sirsa, Haryana, build working Arduino, ESP32 and electronics projects. The lab already exists, with more than ₹50,000 of components plus the 3D printer and computers invested personally. We are looking for a few hardware partners to keep it sustainable, mainly with components rather than cash.

₹50,000+in components, plus the 3D printer & computers, all personally funded

The initiative already exists. Now it needs to scale.

So far, components for about 100 students have been bought out of pocket as a regular customer of these suppliers. A partnership would make the lab sustainable, not start it.

    How we'll use your hardware

    • Every sponsored component goes into student projects in the lab, and none of it is resold.
    • Students build with your hardware, and you receive photos and videos of what they make.
    • We send you a short update on how the components were used.
    • We give you honest feedback on how first-time users get on with your products.
