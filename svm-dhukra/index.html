About the initiative

Students learning engineering by building it.

SVM Dhukra (Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Dhukra) is a small private school in Sirsa, Haryana. Alongside regular classes, its students work on Arduino, electronics, science and robotics projects using real development boards, sensors, motors and a 3D printer.

The initiative is mentored by Sahil Kulria, a robotics engineer and IIT Kanpur graduate. He has personally invested more than ₹50,000 in the lab's components and also provided its 3D printer and computers. He guides students from a first blinking LED to complete working models.

The goal is simple: students from a small town should get the same hands-on engineering exposure as students anywhere else, and learn that they can build technology, not just use it.

Practical STEM learning

Learn it by making it work.

Every project follows the same path real engineers follow, so students learn the process along with the topic.

IdeaStart from a real problem, such as rain, water levels, traffic or safety
CircuitChoose the sensors and wire them on a breadboard
CodeProgram the Arduino or ESP32 and read the sensor data
BuildTurn it into a model, with 3D-printed parts where needed
DebugFind out why it doesn't work yet, then fix it
PresentDemonstrate it and explain how it works
Where we're going

Future robotics development
