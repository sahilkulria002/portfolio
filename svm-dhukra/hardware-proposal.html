Robotics Hardware
Partnership Proposal

Regular component support for a school robotics lab where about 100 students already build Arduino, ESP32 and electronics projects.

SVM Dhukra · Sirsa, Haryana, IndiaPrepared by Sahil Kulria, Robotics Engineer
01 · Summary

The lab exists. We're asking for help sustaining it.

SVM Dhukra is a small private school in Sirsa, Haryana. About 100 students take part in hands-on Arduino, electronics, science and robotics projects. More than ₹50,000 of components has been bought personally to make this possible.

Students use up sensors, jumper wires, motors and filament faster than one person can keep buying them. We are looking for a small number of hardware partners for regular, practical component support, so that every student has parts to build with.

What we're asking for: components, not cash. Start with a one-time contribution of ₹2,000–₹5,000 in components, or a quarterly kit of ₹5,000–₹10,000. All quantities are agreed with you in advance.
