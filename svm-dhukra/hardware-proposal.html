01 · Summary

The lab already exists. Partners help it grow.

SVM Dhukra (Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Dhukra) is a small private school in Sirsa, Haryana. About 100 students take part in hands-on Arduino, electronics, science and robotics projects. More than ₹50,000 of components has been bought personally to make this possible, and the lab's 3D printer and computers were also provided personally.

As more students join and projects get more ambitious, from simple sensors to robotic arms, we would like to work with a few hardware partners who share the goal of putting real engineering in students' hands.

How partners help: with components rather than cash, from ₹5,000 a month. You choose the items from our wishlist to match your catalogue, and we agree the details together.

    How we'll use your hardware

    • Every sponsored component goes into student projects in the lab, and none of it is resold.
    • Students build with your hardware, and you receive photos and videos of what they make.
    • We send you a short update on how the components were used.
    • We give you honest feedback on how first-time users get on with your products.
