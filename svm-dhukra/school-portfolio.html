01 · About SVM Dhukra

A small school with a working robotics lab

SVM Dhukra (Saraswati Vidya Mandir, Dhukra) is a small private school in Sirsa, Haryana. Alongside regular academics, its students spend time in a hands-on lab working on Arduino, electronics, science and robotics projects with real development boards, sensors, motors and a 3D printer.

The initiative is mentored by Sahil Kulria, a robotics engineer from IIT Kanpur who works in the robotics industry. He has personally invested more than ₹50,000 in components, and provided the lab's 3D printer and computers, so that students can learn by building, not just by reading.

Why it matters. Students in small towns rarely get to hold a sensor, wire a circuit or watch their own code move a motor. At SVM Dhukra that is part of normal school life, and it changes what students believe they are capable of building.

04 · Practical learning methodology

Learn it by making it work

Every project follows the same path real engineers follow, so students learn the process along with the topic.

IdeaStart from a real problem: rain, water levels, traffic, safety.
CircuitChoose the sensors and wire them on a breadboard.
CodeProgram the Arduino or ESP32 and read the sensor data.
BuildTurn it into a model, with 3D-printed parts where needed.
DebugFind out why it doesn't work yet, then fix it.
PresentDemonstrate it and explain how it works.
