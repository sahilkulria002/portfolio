Simulation loop: URDF model, Gazebo and RViz are used to check motion and integration before anything runs on the physical robot.
01 · PERCEPTION

3D visual servoing

Using stereo vision and real-time feedback, the arm detects a moving object, estimates its 3D position and keeps following it while it stays within reach.

02 · PLANNING

Motion planning with MoveIt2

Inverse kinematics and trajectory planning turn targets into joint motion the arm can actually execute.

03 · AUTONOMY

Autonomous navigation

The mobile base uses SLAM, LiDAR localisation and sensor fusion to plan paths around obstacles.

04 · CONTROL

Synchronised actuation

The stepper joints are coordinated through the embedded controllers. I handled the hardware–software integration and bring-up myself.

05 · INTERFACES

Teleoperation

The robot can be driven manually through custom UI sliders, a joystick or the keyboard, for testing and direct control.

06 · VALIDATION

Simulation first

I validate navigation, trajectories and integration in Gazebo before deploying to the physical hardware.

I've deliberately left out payload, reach and accuracy figures. They'll be published once they've been measured.

About

Software engineer by training. Robot builder by choice.

I work on robots that have to function in the physical world, where software, sensors, actuators and mechanics all have to work together reliably.

I studied Materials Science and Engineering at IIT Kanpur (B.Tech + M.Tech dual degree), and spent most of my time there in the robotics lab. I led the team that built AUTOMI, the institute's autonomous humanoid robot, and worked on its actuation, control, balancing and navigation.

After graduating I spent two years as a Senior Software Developer at Amadeus, building large-scale data systems. That work taught me how to design software that stays reliable in production.

Today I work in the robotics industry at Wipro PARI. On my own time I'm building a ROS2 mobile manipulator with stereo perception, MoveIt2 planning, SLAM navigation and embedded stepper control, validated in simulation before it touches hardware.

I also care about making robotics accessible. During the first COVID-19 lockdown I helped several schools move to digital classrooms. Today I mentor the robotics lab at SVM Dhukra, where students build Arduino and electronics projects with their own hands.
