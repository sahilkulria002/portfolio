# Outreach: other electronics suppliers and distributors

For Arduino and ESP32 distributors, component stores and **local electronics shops in Sirsa, Hisar or Bathinda**.
**Angle:** a new relationship. Be specific and keep the ask small. For local shops, an in-person visit with a printed proposal works best.
**Best ask:** an educational discount, or a one-time component contribution (₹2,000–₹5,000).
