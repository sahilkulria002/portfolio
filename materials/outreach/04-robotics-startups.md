# Outreach: robotics startups and kit manufacturers

For Indian robotics and STEM-kit startups, and robotics companies with a CSR or community budget.
**Angle:** engineer to engineer. Lead with the technical credibility of the mentor and the lab as a test bed for the company's kits.
**Best ask:** robotics kits, mentorship or workshop support, or samples of the startup's own products to test.

---

## 1. Professional email

**Subject:** A school lab in Haryana that would put your robotics kits to real use

Hi [Name],

I'm Sahil Kulria, a robotics engineer (IIT Kanpur, ROS2 and mobile manipulation) working in the robotics industry. In my own time I mentor a school robotics lab at SVM Dhukra in Sirsa, Haryana, where around 100 students build Arduino, ESP32 and sensor projects.

I've personally funded the lab with more than ₹50,000 in components, a 3D printer, boards and sensors. The next step for the students is moving from single-sensor models to actual robots: smart cars, small arms and multi-sensor systems.

I think there's a good fit with [Startup]:

- **Kits:** a few of your kits used by real first-time users, with structured feedback on what works and what confuses them
- **Workshop:** a one-hour session, online or on-site, from one of your engineers
- **Samples:** new products tested in a real classroom before wider launch

In return, you'd get partner recognition, student project videos built on your hardware, and candid usability notes from a robotics engineer who mentors the students using them.
